### Content Pipeline

- Since most main events already have target dates marked down (see [events.md](events.md) and [hindu-calendar.md](hindu-calendar.md)), batch-produce posts in advance and schedule them so nothing goes out late
- Maintain a running list of ideal post types and content ideas, planned ahead rather than only reactive

### Once Comms Directors Are Hired

Hiring is run by Anishka; see [responsibilities.md](responsibilities.md).

- Give directors their own account access
- Prepare a brand template: colors, fonts, consistent post layouts
- Build a prompt-engineered caption generator: reusable prompt that takes event details and outputs a solid caption
- Produce content in advance using the batch pipeline above
- Anish shifts to approving director posts: quality control, workload / expectations management
