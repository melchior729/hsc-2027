# Budget

**Total club budget: $650** for the year.

This is a **major constraint** on everything we plan: events, food, materials, O-Week, promo. Every spend decision has to be cognizant of this number. Until (and unless) we are ratified, we treat **$650 as the hard ceiling**.

See also: [ratification.md](ratification.md) · [events.md](events.md) · [ideas.md](ideas.md)

## Reimbursement after ratification

If we get ratified under the USC, the university can **refund eligible club spending** when we submit paperwork / receipts. That only kicks in **after** ratification succeeds. Until then, or if ratification fails / goes wrong, we must live inside the $650.
