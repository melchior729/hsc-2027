## Role notes

### Executive Officers (Krishna, Arnav)

Lead club ratification and USC recognition work. May take on other operational tasks as needed. Ratification is their primary mandate this year. See [ratification.md](ratification.md).

### Events (Ishani, Shivan)

Design and run events: format, logistics, space bookings, and day-of execution. See [events.md](events.md).

### External (Nidhi)

Club-to-club and outside relationships: partnerships, co-events, outreach to other student orgs, and representing HSC externally. Often also handles collaboration asks, guest invites, and community / temple connections when needed.

### Internal (Anishka)

Member-facing and club-ops work: membership lists, volunteer coordination, meeting logistics, internal communication, retention, and making sure people feel plugged into the club. Often the glue between execs and general members.

### Finance (Sanmita, Ivneet)

Budgeting, tracking spending, reimbursements, funding applications (USC / faculty / grants), and keeping event costs within approved asks. Sign-off and record-keeping so the club stays clean for ratification and audits. See [budget.md](budget.md).

### Comms (Anish)

Owns regular club Instagram: posts, stories, event promo, festival shoutouts, and normal club content. Separate from the Production Team's higher-production unique videos. See [content.md](content.md).

### Mandir (Praven)

Owns pooja and ritual practice: mantras, ceremony knowledge, and care of the physical Mandir setup. Primary person for the religious / worship side of club activities. See [location.md](location.md).

### Senior Advisor (Himani)

Carryover from last year. Institutional memory and advice. Not day-to-day ops; presidents and VPs can ask for context, past contacts, and what worked / did not.
