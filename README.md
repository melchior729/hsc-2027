## How to Use This

1. Find your tasks: open [responsibilities.md](responsibilities.md) and look for your name / role. That is your current to-do list; it updates as decisions get made.
2. Understand your role: check [structure.md](structure.md) for what your position covers long-term, not just this week's asks.
3. Check the big picture: [tracker.md](tracker.md) shows open work across the whole club, and how the pieces fit together.

Everything else is reference. Files like events, budget, ratification, and location hold the detail behind your tasks. When you need more context, [responsibilities.md](responsibilities.md) links you to the right place.

`@bringup[Name]` tags mean that person specifically needs to weigh in before something is locked. If you see your name, that is your cue to follow up.

Update files as decisions get made. Replace `TBD` with real answers over time. When something changes, update it in one place and let the links carry it everywhere else.
