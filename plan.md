### Role notes

**Executive Officers (Krishna, Arnav)**  
Lead club ratification and USC recognition work. May take on other operational tasks as needed. Ratification is their primary mandate this year.

**Events (Ishani, Shivan)**  
Design and run events: format, logistics, space bookings, and day-of execution.

**External (Nidhi)**  
Club-to-club and outside relationships: partnerships, co-events, outreach to other student orgs, and representing HSC externally. Often also handles collaboration asks, guest invites, and community / temple connections when needed.

**Internal (Anishka)**  
Member-facing and club-ops work: membership lists, volunteer coordination, meeting logistics, internal communication, retention, and making sure people feel plugged into the club. Often the glue between execs and general members.

**Finance (Sanita, Ivneet)**  
Budgeting, tracking spending, reimbursements, funding applications (USC / faculty / grants), and keeping event costs within approved asks. Sign-off and record-keeping so the club stays clean for ratification and audits.

**Comms (Anish)**  
Owns **regular club Instagram**: posts, stories, event promo, festival shoutouts, and normal “what we’re doing” club content. Separate from the Production Team’s higher-production unique videos (see Content below).

**Mandir (Praven)**  
Owns puja and ritual practice: mantras, ceremony knowledge, and care of the physical Mandir setup. Primary person for religious / worship side of club activities.

**Senior Advisor (Himani)**  
Carryover from last year — institutional memory and advice. Not day-to-day ops; presidents and VPs can ask for context, past contacts, and what worked / didn’t.

## Current space

HSC’s main operations sit in Western’s **Multifaith Room** — a shared space open to people of other faiths as well.

- We keep a **physical Mandir** in a tall closet / cupboard there: open it and the murti, ritual items, and related materials are set up inside.
- Because the room is shared, scheduling clashes happen (our practice vs. other groups). That’s unfair on both sides and is part of why a dedicated Hindu space would help.

**Praven** looks after this Mandir setup day to day.

## Budget

**Total club budget: $650** for the year.

This is a **major constraint** on everything we plan — events, food, materials, O-Week, promo. Every spend decision has to be cognizant of this number. Until (and unless) we are ratified, we treat **$650 as the hard ceiling**.

### Reimbursement after ratification
If we get ratified under the USC, the university can **refund eligible club spending** when we submit paperwork / receipts. That only kicks in **after** ratification succeeds. Until then — or if ratification fails / goes wrong — we must live inside the $650.

- **Owners (day-to-day):** Finance (Sanita, Ivneet)
